# Battery test — 7 September 2026

Source: `2026-09-07.csv`, 104 records retrieved over USB without resetting or
clearing the device. Sequence numbers are contiguous. Records 1–4 are setup
checks on USB; the battery session starts at record 5. Times below are BST.

Between 11:16:38 and 16:22:22 the gauge fell from 100% to 43% over 5h 05m 44s.
The first USB-powered record at 16:24:06 reported 42%; exclude that final
interval from battery-only comparisons because cable insertion time is unknown.

Awake duration uses uptime differences within a boot. Sleep duration uses wall
clock differences between recorded events, and includes wake/startup overhead
before the first sample. Small timing differences reflect integer-second epoch
resolution. These are changes in a fuel-gauge estimate, not measured energy
attribution: gauge lag, voltage relaxation, and coarse percentage resolution can
move reported loss between interval categories.

Brightness was 204/255 (80%) throughout screen-on records. Most wake sessions
lasted about 122–127 seconds, consistent with the existing two-minute timeout;
one lasted about four minutes and another about three. Most recorded screen
activity was the Status page. The lower factory defaults have not replaced the
user's saved brightness/timeout settings.

Only one Wi-Fi scan (2.790 seconds) and one BLE scan (4.170 seconds) were
recorded, together constituting one feeding sweep. No non-idle battle states
were recorded. This was primarily an intermittent screen-check/standby test,
not a reproduction of the reported 30–45-minute heavy-use case. Software radio
states do not prove that a radio or its controller was completely powered down.

Sleep/wake sequencing shows deep-sleep resumes for boots 5–18, with no logged
cold boot interrupting the battery-only sequence. It does not establish actual
sleep current or whether board peripheral supply rails were disabled. Even the
longest interval (1h 55m) showed a 12-point decrease before the next wake.

Next useful comparison: repeat with lower brightness and a 15-second timeout
(or sleep immediately after checking). Separately inspect peripheral power
rails and measure sleeping battery current if hardware is available. Avoid
predicting full runtime by extrapolating this partial percentage trace: discharge
is visibly non-uniform, particularly toward the end. No firmware/settings changes
were made during this retrieval and analysis.
